# 2. Linear Models and Overfitting

Predictive modelling does not start with a new kind of mathematics. It starts with the linear model
you already know, and a different question asked of it: not *is this association real?* but *how
close will my guess be for the next person?*

Asking that question exposes a problem immediately — one that no amount of statistical training
protects you from, and that this book spends the next three chapters dealing with.

**In this chapter:**

- **[Linear Models: a Brief Theory](theory_linear_models.md)** — least squares, predictions, and
  the two different questions a fitted model can answer.
- **[Linear Models in Action](practice_linear_models.ipynb)** — fitting models in python, and
  meeting the first villain: overfitting.
- **[The First Villain: Overfitting](overfitting_ex.ipynb)** — overfitting in other disguises, from
  polynomials to Google Flu Trends to Ptolemy.
