# 2. Linear Models and Overfitting

Predictive modelling starts with the linear model you already know, and with a different question
asked of it: instead of *is this association real?*, we ask *how close will the guess be for the
next person?*

Asking the question that way exposes a problem almost immediately, one that no amount of statistical
training protects against, and that the next three chapters are devoted to.

**In this chapter:**

- **[Linear Models: a Brief Theory](theory_linear_models.md)**: least squares, predictions, and the
  two rather different questions a fitted model can answer.
- **[Linear Models in Action](practice_linear_models.ipynb)**: fitting models in python, and a first
  encounter with overfitting.
- **[Overfitting in Other Guises](overfitting_ex.ipynb)**: the same failure in less obvious costume,
  from polynomials to Google Flu Trends to Ptolemy.
